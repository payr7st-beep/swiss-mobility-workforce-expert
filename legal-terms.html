03 Description of the Service

SMWE is a declarative AI agent built on Microsoft Copilot Studio and Azure infrastructure. It provides compliance guidance and regulatory interpretation in the following domains:

Immigration (D05)
Permit classification, EU/EFTA and third-country national tracks, quota monitoring under Swiss immigration law (AIG, VZAE, AuG).
Global Mobility (D06)
Assignment categories, Certificate of Coverage (A1), shadow payroll guidance, permanent establishment risk awareness.
Workforce Compliance (D07)
Swiss Code of Obligations (OR), Labour Act (ArG), collective labour agreements (CLAs), employee participation law.
Staffing Advisory (D08)
Read-only guidance on the Temporary Staffing Act (AVG) and staffing compliance. Does not constitute a staffing or recruitment service.
Expert Network (D10)
Referrals to qualified immigration lawyers, international tax advisors, and Swiss fiduciaries (Treuhänder). Referrals are informational only.

All rule sets embedded in the Service are derived from publicly available Swiss and EU legislation. Statutory text is reproduced pursuant to Swiss URG Art. 5 and the EU's open reuse policy for EUR-Lex publications. Proprietary or annotated third-party commentary is not reproduced.
