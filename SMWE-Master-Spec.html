Product Overview

What is SMWE?

The Swiss Mobility & Workforce Expert (SMWE) is a declarative AI agent built for Microsoft Copilot Studio, providing authoritative compliance guidance on Swiss workforce mobility, immigration, cross-border employment, global assignments, social security agreements, and international tax coordination.

SMWE is the third member of the SevenSprings Swiss Compliance Expert Suite — a family of specialist AI agents covering the full Swiss employment compliance landscape.

What SMWE does

Compliance guidance · Regulatory interpretation · Decision support on immigration, mobility, and cross-border work

Who it serves

HR professionals · Global mobility teams · Payroll managers · Corporate counsel · Fiduciaries · Employment lawyers

What it does NOT do

Tax opinions · Legal filings · Immigration applications · Payroll calculations (→ SPE) · Staffing services (AVG-regulated)

Deployment

Microsoft Copilot Studio declarative agent · Microsoft 365 · Microsoft Teams · AppSource Marketplace

Four-Layer Update Mechanism

Layer 1 — Gap Detection (Real-Time)

Query log scanned every 24h. Low-quality answers flagged and clustered into topic gaps. Admin dashboard surfaces authoring backlog.

gapDetector.js — runs via GitHub Actions cron 01:00 CET

Layer 2 — Regulatory Monitoring (Monthly)

Monthly scan of SEM, SECO, BSV, ZAS, SIF, ESTV for regulatory changes. High-frequency watch list with defined triggers.

Layer 3 — Quarterly KB Review

Full knowledge-base review every quarter. Expert sign-off required before modules marked reviewed: true. Version increment deployed.

Layer 4 — Annual Major Refresh

Complete alignment with current-year legal sources. All figures, thresholds, quota numbers updated. Major version increment.
