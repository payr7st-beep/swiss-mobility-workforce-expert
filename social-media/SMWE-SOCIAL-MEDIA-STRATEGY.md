## 2. LinkedIn Strategy (Primary Channel)

**Rationale:** LinkedIn is the primary channel for HR professionals, Global Mobility specialists, employment lawyers, payroll managers, and fiduciaries — SMWE's core audience.

### Content pillars

**Pillar 1 — Educational posts (40% of content)**
Short, structured posts teaching one mobility/immigration rule per post.

Examples:
- "The 183-day rule explained in 60 seconds — and why it doesn't protect you as much as you think"
- "France vs Germany vs Italy: the 3 different home-office rules for Swiss cross-border workers"
- "What is an A1 certificate and when do you actually need one?"
- "G permit vs B permit: which permit for your cross-border employee?"
- "Shadow payroll: what it is, when you need it, how it works"

**Pillar 2 — Regulatory updates (25% of content)**
Quick-turnaround posts when regulatory changes happen.

Examples:
- "NEW: Switzerland-Italy frontalier agreement 2023 — what changed and who is affected"
- "2026 Swiss immigration quotas: L 4,000 / B 4,500 for third-country nationals — what employers need to know"
- "SECO update: 84 extended CLAs in force as of 1 July 2026"

**Pillar 3 — Product updates (15% of content)**
Milestone announcements, new knowledge modules, feature releases.

Examples:
- "SMWE is now live on Microsoft AppSource — try it free"
- "New in SMWE: DTA country matrix — 100+ countries in one tool"
- "Introducing SMWE + SPE cross-product integration: from work permit to payroll in one session"

**Pillar 4 — Case scenarios / Q&A (15% of content)**
Anonymised scenario-based posts demonstrating SMWE's value.

Examples:
- "Scenario: German resident, Swiss employer, 3 days home office per week. What's the compliance checklist?"
- "Reader question: does our Indian assignee need an A1 certificate for a 2-day conference in Zurich?"

**Pillar 5 — Community & Partnerships (5% of content)**
Association mentions, partner shoutouts, industry events.

### Posting cadence
- **LinkedIn:** 4–5 posts per week
- Tuesday and Thursday: educational posts (highest engagement days for B2B LinkedIn)
- Monday: regulatory update (if any) or product news
- Wednesday: scenario/Q&A
- Friday: lighter insight or quote card

### LinkedIn page
- Company page: SevenSprings Technology AG
- Product page / showcase page: Swiss Mobility & Workforce Expert
- Creator mode on key founder/team profiles for personal amplification

### LinkedIn ads (paid)
- Sponsored content: promote top-performing organic posts
- Message ads: targeted at HR Directors, Global Mobility Managers, Employment Lawyers in CH, DE, FR, IT, UK
- Targeting: Job title, company size (50+), industry (all), geography (Switzerland + border regions)
- Budget: CHF 500–1,500/month at launch

## 3. Instagram Strategy (Secondary Channel)

**Rationale:** Instagram reaches HR professionals through visual content; effective for brand awareness and driving traffic to LinkedIn/product.

### Content format
- **Carousel posts:** "3 things every employer with cross-border workers must know"
- **Infographics:** Visual explainers of the 183-day rule, A1 flowchart, permit comparison chart
- **Quote cards:** Key compliance rules in visual format
- **Reels (short video):** 30–60 second explainers ("What is shadow payroll?")

### Posting cadence
- **Instagram:** 3 posts per week + 5 Stories per week
- Reels: 1 per week (highest reach potential)

### Visual style
- Clean, white-dominant design with Swiss Red accents
- Data visualisation style (charts, process flows, comparison tables)
- Consistent branded frame and SMWE logo watermark
- Multilingual: alternate German and English captions

## 4. Facebook Strategy (Supporting Channel)

**Rationale:** Facebook reaches HR communities, Swiss expatriate groups, and cross-border worker communities.

### Target groups
- Swiss HR & Payroll professionals
- Cross-border workers (Grenzgänger) groups (FR-CH, DE-CH, IT-CH)
- Expats in Switzerland
- Swiss employer groups

### Content format
- Repurposed LinkedIn educational posts
- Longer-form articles shared from LinkedIn
- Community Q&A (answer questions in relevant FB groups)
- Product announcements

### Posting cadence
- **Facebook:** 2–3 posts per week
- Priority: sharing top LinkedIn posts; group engagement

## 5. X.com (Twitter) Strategy

**Rationale:** X.com is used by Swiss regulatory bodies, legal commentators, global mobility media, and tech-forward HR professionals.

### Content format
- Quick regulatory updates (link to detailed LinkedIn post)
- SMWE launch announcements and milestones
- Engagement with @SECO_CH, @sem_online, @BSV_OFAS
- Retweet/engage with Swiss global mobility media

### Posting cadence
- **X.com:** 2–3 posts per day (including retweets and replies)
- Priority: real-time regulatory reactions and product news
