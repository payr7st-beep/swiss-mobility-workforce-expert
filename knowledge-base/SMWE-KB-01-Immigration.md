## 1. Swiss Immigration Framework

Swiss immigration law distinguishes between two primary categories:

### A. EU/EFTA Nationals
Governed by the **Agreement on the Free Movement of Persons (AFMP)** with EU member states and the EFTA Convention for Iceland, Liechtenstein, and Norway.

Rights include:
- Right to seek and take up employment
- Right to reside for employment purposes
- Right to remain after employment
- Family reunification rights
- Access to social security coordination under EU Reg. 883/2004

Permit types for EU/EFTA:
- **G Permit (cross-border commuter):** Weekly return to home country required; no quota for EU/EFTA
- **L Permit (short-term):** Up to 12 months; no quota for EU/EFTA nationals with contract
- **B Permit (annual):** Renewable annually; no quota for EU/EFTA nationals
- **C Permit (settlement):** After 5 years (EU/EFTA) or 10 years (third-country); no quota

### B. Third-Country Nationals (Non-EU/EFTA)
Governed by the **AIG (Ausländer- und Integrationsgesetz / Federal Act on Foreign Nationals and Integration)** and **VZAE (Verordnung über Zulassung, Aufenthalt und Erwerbstätigkeit / Ordinance on Admission, Stay and Employment)**.

Subject to:
- Federal annual quotas
- Cantonal sub-quotas
- Precedence of Swiss nationals and EU/EFTA nationals (domestic labour market test)
- SECO/SEM approval requirements

## 2. Permit Types — Detailed Reference

### G Permit — Cross-Border Commuter Permit
**IMM-005**

- **Eligibility:** Nationals of EU/EFTA border states (DE, FR, IT, AT, LI) living in border zones who return home at least weekly; also available to non-EU/EFTA nationals meeting conditions
- **Validity:** 5 years for EU/EFTA; annually renewable for third-country nationals
- **Condition:** Employee must return to home country at least once per week (Wochenaufenthalter)
- **Work location:** Typically in a border canton; non-border work requires assessment
- **Home office:** See KB Module 03 (Cross-Border Work) for home office rules
- **No quota** for EU/EFTA cross-border commuters

### L Permit — Short-Term Residence Permit
**IMM-004**

- **Validity:** Up to 12 months (extendable in some cases to 24 months)
- **Purpose:** Employment with fixed-term contract; training; internships
- **EU/EFTA:** No quota; administrative notification sufficient for short stays
- **Third-country:** Subject to annual federal quota (4,000 for 2026)
- **Key rule:** Employer must demonstrate labour market test (unless EU/EFTA)

### B Permit — Annual Residence Permit
**IMM-002**

- **Validity:** 1 year, renewable annually (EU/EFTA); 1 year with right to renewal (third-country with valid employment)
- **Pathway to C:** EU/EFTA after 5 years; third-country after 10 years ordinary residence
- **Third-country quota 2026:** 4,500 B permits available
- **Conditions:** Valid employment contract; accommodation; no reliance on social assistance

### C Permit — Settlement Permit
**IMM-003**

- **Validity:** Unlimited; renewable every 5 years (EU/EFTA) or every 10 years (third-country)
- **Eligibility:** After 5 continuous years on B permit (EU/EFTA) or 10 years (third-country)
- **Rights:** Near-equivalent to Swiss nationals for employment purposes; no labour market test
- **Revocation:** Possible for criminal convictions, long-term absence, or sustained welfare dependency

### Notification Procedure
**IMM-006**

For EU/EFTA service providers with contracts up to 90 days per calendar year:
- Online notification via EURES/cantonal portals
- No permit required for first 90 working days per year
- Subject to posted-worker rules (minimum wages, working conditions)
- A1 certificate required for social security purposes

## 4. Labour Market Test

For third-country nationals, employers must demonstrate:

1. **Priority search:** Position was advertised through RAV (Regional Employment Centres) for a minimum period
2. **No qualified Swiss/EU candidates available:** Documentary evidence required
3. **Role meets qualification threshold:** Specialist or senior management position
4. **Salary at market rate:** Prevents wage undercutting; SECO monitors

**Exceptions** (partial or full exemption from labour market test):
- Intra-company transferees (ICT)
- Researchers and academics
- Certain executive roles
- Reciprocity-based agreements

---

## 5. Posted Workers to Switzerland

Foreign companies sending workers to Switzerland must comply with:

- **Posted Workers Act (EntsG):** Minimum wages, working conditions, and notification obligations
- **Online notification:** Via SECO notification platform, 8 days before work commences (general rule)
- **Documents to retain:** Posted worker contracts, payslips, social security documentation
- **A1 certificate:** Must be available on-site upon request

Sectors with highest posted-worker activity: construction, cleaning, catering, IT services.

## 6. Family Reunification
**IMM-007**

Conditions for accompanying family members:
- Spouse/registered partner
- Children under 18 (up to 25 if in education in some cases)
- Cohabiting partners (B/C permit holders)

**EU/EFTA holders:** Family reunification right under AFMP regardless of family member nationality.

**Third-country B/C holders:** Family reunification permitted; financial self-sufficiency required; integration conditions may apply after extended period.

---

## 7. Permit Renewals
**IMM-008**

- **B Permit (EU/EFTA):** Renewal generally automatic if employment continues; apply 3 months before expiry at cantonal migration office
- **B Permit (third-country):** Renewal subject to continued employment, conduct, and financial independence; apply 6 months before expiry
- **G Permit:** Renewal requires evidence of continued cross-border commute; employment contract update required
- **C Permit:** Re-registration every 5 or 10 years; essentially automatic absent criminal or welfare issues
