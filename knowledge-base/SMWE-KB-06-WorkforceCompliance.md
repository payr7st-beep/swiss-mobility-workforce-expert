## A. Employment Contract Law (OR Art. 319–362)

### A.1 Formation and Types

| Contract type | Key features | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Permanent (unbefristet) | No end date; terminates only by notice or agreement | OR Art. 319 |
| Fixed-term (befristet) | Ends automatically on expiry; repeated renewal can convert to permanent | OR Art. 334 |
| Trial period (Probezeit) | Default: 1 month; extendable by agreement to max 3 months | OR Art. 335b |
| Part-time | Pro-rata rights identical to full-time; written clarity recommended | OR Art. 319 |
| On-call (Abrufarbeit) | If average hours are predictable, notice obligations apply | OR Art. 319 |

**Form:** Employment contracts may be oral or written. Written form is required for specific clauses (non-competition, restraint of trade, fixed-term extensions). SMWE recommends written form for all employment relationships.

### A.2 Notice Periods (OR Art. 335a–335c)

| Tenure | Notice period (minimum, statutory) |
|---|---|
| During trial period (up to 3 months) | 7 days |
| Year 1 of employment | 1 month (end of calendar month) |
| Years 2–9 | 2 months (end of calendar month) |
| Year 10 onwards | 3 months (end of calendar month) |

**Contractual extension:** Parties may agree to longer notice periods; both parties must have equal or proportionate notice periods. Collective labour agreements (CLAs / GAV) may extend statutory minimums — always check applicable CLA.

**Termination protection periods (Sperrfristen, OR Art. 336c):** Notice given or effective during the following periods is void:

- First 30 days of illness/accident (year 1); 90 days (years 2–5); 180 days (year 6+)
- Military/civil service / civil protection duty
- Pregnancy and 16 weeks post-birth
- Foreign aid service up to 12 weeks per year

### A.3 Wrongful Dismissal (OR Art. 336–336b)

Dismissal is abusive (*missbräuchlich*) if motivated by:
- Personal characteristics unrelated to work (origin, religion, political views, sexual orientation)
- Exercising constitutionally protected rights (e.g. labour association)
- Domestic obligations (family care, maternity)
- Whistleblowing in good faith

**Remedy:** Maximum 6 months' salary. Not reinstatement — Switzerland has no reinstatement obligation (unlike EU jurisdictions).

**Mass dismissals (OR Art. 335d–335g):** Employer must consult employee representatives and notify cantonal labour office when dismissing:
- ≥10 employees within 30 days (companies 20–99 employees)
- ≥10% of workforce (companies 100–299)
- ≥30 employees (companies 300+)

30-day waiting period before dismissals take effect; severance obligation may arise.

### A.4 Restraint of Trade / Non-Competition (OR Art. 340–340c)

| Condition | Rule |
|---|---|
| Scope | Limited to knowledge of customers, trade secrets, or manufacturing processes acquired in employment |
| Maximum duration | 3 years (exceptionally) |
| Geographic/functional scope | Must be reasonably limited |
| Compensation | Statute is silent; courts increasingly require financial consideration for lengthy clauses |
| Enforceability | Courts apply proportionality; overly broad clauses are reduced, not voided |

## B. Working Time Law (ArG + ArGV1/2/3)

### B.1 Scope of the Labour Act (ArG)

ArG applies to all enterprises in Switzerland except:
- Public sector (federal, cantonal, communal administrations)
- Agriculture, domestic service, transport (separate statutes)
- Senior management (leitende Angestellte) exempt from rest/working time provisions

### B.2 Maximum Working Hours (ArG Art. 9)

| Sector | Weekly maximum |
|---|---|
| Industrial enterprises | 45 hours |
| All other enterprises (incl. office workers, sales) | 50 hours |

**Overtime:** Hours above contractual time but below statutory maximum = overtime (*Überzeit*). Hours above statutory maximum = excess hours (*Überstunden*). Both require compensation (pay supplement or time off in lieu).

| Excess type | Compensation |
|---|---|
| Überstunden (above statutory max) | 25% wage supplement; cannot be waived except in writing for senior staff |
| Überzeit (above contract, below statutory max) | As agreed; default: time off in lieu or salary |

### B.3 Rest Periods (ArG Art. 15, 18, 22)

| Rest type | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Daily rest | 11 consecutive hours between shifts |
| Weekly rest | 35 consecutive hours (including Sunday where possible) |
| Meal break | 15 min (shift ≤ 5.5h); 30 min (> 7h); 1 hour (> 9h) |
| Sunday work | Permitted only with cantonal/SECO authorisation; 50% wage supplement |
| Night work (23:00–06:00) | Authorisation required; 10% time supplement or equivalent pay |

### B.4 Special Categories

**Pregnant employees and nursing mothers:**
- No night work, Sunday work, or overtime from notification of pregnancy
- Light duties on request from month 8; reduced to 4 hours/day from month 8 if requested
- 8 weeks maternity rest after birth (minimum); 16 weeks total maternity leave under EOG

**Young workers (under 18):**
- Maximum 9 hours/day; no night work; no Sunday work without authorisation
- Separate protective provisions under ArG Art. 29–32

**Home office:** Not explicitly covered by ArG; SECO guidance: ArG protections apply to home office where employer can verify compliance. Working time records required even for home office.

## C. Collective Labour Agreements (CLAs / GAV)

### C.1 Legal Framework

**AVV (Allgemeinverbindlicherklärung / Declaration of General Applicability):** When SECO declares a CLA generally binding (*allgemeinverbindlich*), it applies to all employers in the sector/region regardless of union membership.

**84 extended CLAs as of 1 August 2026 (SECO register):** All employers in covered sectors must comply, even if not members of the employer association that negotiated the CLA.

### C.2 Key Extended CLAs by Sector (Selected)

| Sector | CLA name | Key provisions |
|---|---|---|
| Construction | LMV Bauhauptgewerbe | Minimum wages by trade/region; 42-hour week; bad weather compensation |
| Electrical installation | LMAV Elektroinstallationsgewerbe | Minimum wages; travel time rules; training levy |
| Painting/plastering | LMAV Maler- und Gipsergewerbe | Regional minimum wages; holiday fund contributions |
| Cleaning/building services | L-GAV Gebäudereinigung | Minimum wages; shift premiums; uniform provision |
| Gastronomy/hotels | L-GAV Gastgewerbe | Minimum wages; service charge rules; accommodation deductions |
| Retail (nationally) | Various cantonal GAVs | Minimum wages vary by canton; Sunday work rules |
| Temporary employment | GAV Personalverleih | Minimum wages; equal treatment principle (after 3 months = client employer standard) |
| Transport (road) | AETR-compliant provisions | Driver rest times; tachograph requirements |
| Private security | GAV Sicherheitsdienste | Minimum wages; night/weekend premiums |
| Cleaning services (national) | L-GAV Reinigungsbranche | Tiered wages by function; social fund contributions |

**Employer obligation:** Determine which CLA(s) apply to each employee before engagement. The SECO CLA register (www.seco.admin.ch/gav) is the authoritative source.

### C.3 Minimum Wage Interaction

Switzerland has no federal statutory minimum wage (unlike EU/UK). Effective minimum wages are:
- **CLA minimums** — if a generally binding CLA applies (see C.2)
- **Cantonal minimums** — Geneva (CHF 24.32/hr, 2026), Jura, Basel-Stadt, Neuchâtel, Ticino, Vaud have enacted cantonal minimums
- **Posted-worker minimums** — foreign employers posting workers to Switzerland must pay the applicable CLA minimum (EntsG, SMWE KB-03 §D)

## D. Employee Participation Plans (ESTV KS37 / KS37A)

### D.1 Legal Framework

**ESTV Circular KS37 (2012, updated 2023):** Governs taxation of employee equity participation — applies to shares, options, RSUs, phantom awards, and all equity-equivalent instruments.

**ESTV KS37A (2024):** Clarifications on international allocation for mobile employees; split-vesting treatment; departure/arrival rules.

**OR Art. 322d–322f:** Statutory basis for employment-linked equity; disclosure obligations in employment contract.

### D.2 Instrument Types and Tax Treatment

| Instrument | Taxable event | Tax base | Canton / Federal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Restricted shares (Sperrfrist ≤ 5yr) | Grant date (with discount for restriction) | FMV less restriction discount (per KS37 table) | Income tax (federal + cantonal) |
| Unrestricted shares | Grant date | FMV at grant | Income tax |
| Vested options (tradeable) | Grant date | Option value (Black-Scholes or comparable) | Income tax |
| Non-vested options (not tradeable) | Exercise date | Spread (FMV at exercise minus strike price) | Income tax |
| RSUs / PSUs | Vesting date | FMV at vesting | Income tax |
| Phantom / SAR (cash-settled) | Payment date | Cash amount received | Income tax |

### D.3 International Allocation (KS37A)

For **mobile employees** who were not Swiss tax resident throughout the entire vesting/restriction period:

**Swiss allocation = (Swiss work days during vesting period ÷ Total calendar days in vesting period) × Taxable spread/value**

**Key rules:**
- SECO "performance period" starts at grant (shares/options) or beginning of vesting schedule (RSUs)
- Work days tracked in accordance with DTA provisions — treaty-exempt foreign work days reduce Swiss allocation
- Employer must maintain day-count records; failure triggers 100% Swiss assessment
- Certificate of source deduction (Bescheinigung Quellensteuer) required for mobile employees subject to withholding

### D.4 Employer Reporting Obligations

| Obligation | Deadline | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Lohnausweis entry (field 5) | With annual wage statement | Cantonal tax authority |
| KS37 employee certificate | At taxable event | Provide to employee for tax return |
| Withholding (QST) on equity events | Within 30 days of taxable event | Cantonal tax authority |
| Departure trigger — unvested options | At departure date | Deemed exercise at departure FMV for QST purposes |

### D.5 Social Security on Equity Compensation

**AHV/ALV treatment (BSV):** Equity-related income is subject to AHV/ALV contributions as *massgebender Lohn* at the taxable event (same timing as income tax event per KS37). Employer must include in monthly AHV declaration.

Exception: Value of RSUs/options allocated to foreign work periods (KS37A) is excluded from Swiss AHV base if the employee was subject to foreign legislation for that period.

## E. Staffing / Temporary Employment (AVG / AVV)

### E.1 Licencing Requirements

**AVG (Arbeitsvermittlungsgesetz):** Employers who place workers with third-party client enterprises (*Personalverleih*) require:
- **Federal licence** (SECO) for cross-cantonal or international placement
- **Cantonal licence** for placement within a single canton
- **No licence required:** internal group transfers, secondments within an enterprise group (with conditions)

**SMWE boundary:** SMWE provides guidance on AVG compliance requirements only. SMWE does not provide staffing or placement services (AVG-regulated; requires licence). See content policy.

### E.2 Key AVG Obligations

| Obligation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Written assignment contract | Required before start of each placement; must specify duration, workplace, remuneration |
| Equal treatment (Gleichstellung) | After 3 months at client enterprise: temp worker entitled to same wages as direct employees in equivalent role |
| Social insurance | Temp agency = employer for all AHV/ALV/UVG obligations |
| CLA — Personalverleih | Mandatory CLA for temp agencies applies minimum wages by function/qualification |
| Maximum placement duration | No statutory maximum in federal law; some cantonal CLAs limit to 24 months |

## F. Compliance Checklist — Employer Onboarding a New Employee in Switzerland

1. **Determine employment type** — permanent, fixed-term, part-time, on-call
2. **Draft employment contract** — include probation, notice, non-competition, confidentiality, equity participation if applicable
3. **Identify applicable CLA(s)** — check SECO register; verify sector and canton; apply minimum wages and working conditions
4. **Verify work authorisation** — permit type per SMWE KB-01; confirm validity before first day
5. **Register with social insurance** — AHV/ALV/UVG registration with cantonal Ausgleichskasse; BVG enrolment with pension fund; SUVA or private UVG insurer
6. **Set up payroll** — apply correct deductions (AHV/IV/EO/ALV/UVG/BVG); configure withholding tax (QST) if applicable; prepare Lohnausweis
7. **Working time records** — implement system for recording actual hours; obligation applies to ArG-covered employees; home office included
8. **Data protection** — employee data subject to nDSG; employee privacy notice required; no processing of sensitive data (health, union) without legal basis
9. **Equity compensation** — if applicable, document vesting schedule, grant FMV, allocation methodology per KS37A from day one
