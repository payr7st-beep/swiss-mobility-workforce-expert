## A. Two-Track System: EU/EFTA vs. Third-Country Nationals

### Track 1 — EU/EFTA Nationals (AFMP)

**Legal basis:** Agreement on Free Movement of Persons (AFMP) SR 0.142.112.681 and EFTA Convention.

**Right of access:** EU/EFTA nationals have the right to seek employment and take up work in Switzerland without a labour market test. No employer needs to demonstrate priority search.

**Permits by duration:**

| Duration of stay/work | Permit type | Quota |
|---|---|---|
| Daily/weekly cross-border commute (returns home weekly) | G Permit | None |
| Up to 12 months (contract ≤ 12 months) | L Permit | None |
| More than 12 months (open-ended or > 12-month contract) | B Permit | None |
| After 5 years of B permit | C Permit (settlement) | N/A — rights-based |

**Key rule (EU/EFTA):** Employer notifies the cantonal migration office. For stays < 90 days / year: notification only (no permit required for EU/EFTA service providers). For longer stays: permit application to cantonal migration office.

### Track 2 — Third-Country Nationals (Non-EU/EFTA)

**Legal basis:** AIG (SR 142.20), VZAE (SR 142.201).

**Conditions for admission:**
1. No qualified Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate available (labour market priority — Inländervorrang)
2. Employer quota available (federal annual allocation, with cantonal sub-quotas)
3. Role qualifies as specialist or executive
4. Salary at market rate (prevents wage undercutting; SECO monitors)
5. SEM / cantonal migration office approval

## B. Permit Classification Decision Tree

```
Step 1: Nationality?
├── EU/EFTA national → Track 1 (AFMP rules; no quota)
│   ├── Weekly cross-border commute? → G Permit
│   ├── Contract ≤ 12 months? → L Permit
│   └── Contract > 12 months? → B Permit
│
└── Non-EU/EFTA (third-country) → Track 2 (AIG/VZAE; quota applies)
    ├── Quota available? → Proceed to permit application
    │   ├── Short-term (≤ 12 months) → L Permit
    │   ├── Long-term (> 12 months) → B Permit
    │   └── Intra-company transfer → B Permit (ICT category)
    └── No quota available → Wait for next quota release or re-assess Q2
```

## C. Permit Type Reference

### G Permit — Grenzgängerausweis / Permis G (Cross-Border Commuter)
**IMM-005**

| Feature | Rule |
|---|---|
| Eligibility — EU/EFTA | National of a neighbouring state with registered residence in border zone; returns home at least once per week |
| Eligibility — TCN | Non-EU/EFTA nationals may also hold G permit under specific bilateral conditions |
| Validity — EU/EFTA | 5 years (renewable) |
| Validity — TCN | 1 year (renewable annually) |
| Quota | None for EU/EFTA; quota may apply for TCN |
| Weekly return obligation | Employee must return to country of residence at least once per week |
| Home-office rule | See SMWE KB-03 (Cross-Border) — complex rules by country |
| Workplace | Typically border cantons; cross-cantonal work requires re-assessment |

**G permit: automatic social security consequence:** G permit holders working in Switzerland are subject to Swiss AHV/ALV unless home-country working time exceeds 49.9% (see Rule Set 1).

### L Permit — Kurzaufenthaltsbewilligung / Autorisation de courte durée
**IMM-004**

| Feature | Rule |
|---|---|
| Duration | Up to 12 months; in exceptional cases renewable up to 24 months |
| EU/EFTA | No quota; administrative notification |
| TCN | Federal quota applies; 2026 allocation: 4,000 (TCN) + 3,000 (EU/EFTA on assignment) |
| Purpose | Fixed-term employment; training; internship; seasonal (seasonal: specific rules) |
| Labour market test | Required for TCN; not required for EU/EFTA |
| Path to B permit | L holders may upgrade to B if contract extended beyond 12 months |

### B Permit — Aufenthaltsbewilligung / Autorisation de séjour
**IMM-002**

| Feature | Rule |
|---|---|
| Duration | 1 year; renewable annually |
| EU/EFTA | No quota; renewable automatically while employed |
| TCN | Federal quota applies; 2026 allocation: 4,500 (TCN) + 500 (EU/EFTA on assignment) |
| Conditions | Valid employment; accommodation; no sustained welfare dependency |
| Path to C | After 5 years continuous B permit (EU/EFTA); 10 years (TCN) |
| Labour market test | Required for TCN at initial application; not at renewal if employed |

### C Permit — Niederlassungsbewilligung / Autorisation d'établissement
**IMM-003**

| Feature | Rule |
|---|---|
| Duration | Unlimited; must be re-registered every 5 years (EU/EFTA) or 10 years (TCN) |
| Eligibility — EU/EFTA | After 5 continuous years on B permit; good standing required |
| Eligibility — TCN | After 10 years on B permit; language/integration requirements |
| Employment rights | Near-equivalent to Swiss nationals; no labour market test; no quota |
| Revocation triggers | Criminal conviction (threshold varies); sustained welfare dependency; long-term absence |

### Notification Procedure (90-day rule)
**IMM-006**

For EU/EFTA service providers with work contracts up to 90 days per calendar year:
- Online notification via cantonal EURES/SEM platform before work begins
- No permit required
- But: posted-worker rules apply (minimum wages, ArG working conditions)
- A1 certificate required if social security implications

## D. Labour Market Test — Procedure for TCN

### Step 1: Pre-publication
Employer verifies that the role cannot be filled by a qualified Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate.

### Step 2: RAV registration
Position advertised through the Regional Employment Centre (RAV / Office régional de placement) for a defined period (typically 5 days under current regulations; SECO monitors compliance).

### Step 3: Documentation
- Proof of RAV advertisement
- Evidence of candidate search and interviews conducted
- Confirmation that no qualified Swiss/EU/EFTA candidate was found
- Job description confirming specialist/executive character

### Step 4: Cantonal authority application
Application to cantonal migration office (Migrationsamt / Service de la population):
- Employer application form
- Employment contract
- Candidate CV and qualifications
- Labour market test documentation
- Salary confirmation (must be at market rate per SECO salary standards)

### Step 5: SECO approval (for some categories)
SECO must approve for certain sectors; cantonal authority coordinates.

## F. Post-Brexit: UK Nationals

**Status:** The UK left the EU; AFMP no longer applies. A separate bilateral framework governs.

| Feature | Rule |
|---|---|
| Permit type | L or B permit (subject to UK-specific quota) |
| Quota | Separate UK allocation (not part of EU/EFTA allocation) |
| Labour market test | Required (same as TCN nationals) |
| Social security | UK-Switzerland Social Security Protocol (not EU 883/2004) |
| A1 equivalent | Certificate of Coverage under bilateral Protocol |
