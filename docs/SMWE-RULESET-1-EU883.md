## A. Core Principles (Reg. 883 Title I)

- **Single applicable legislation (Art. 11(1)):** A person in scope is subject to the legislation of ONE member state only. This is the anti-double-charge and anti-gap anchor of all cross-border payroll assessment.
- **Equal treatment (Art. 5):** Foreign benefits, income, facts, and events occurring in another state must be treated as domestic equivalents.
- **Aggregation (Art. 6):** Insurance, employment, self-employment, and residence periods completed under another member state's legislation count as domestic where rights acquisition, retention, or access depends on periods.
- **Exportability (Art. 7):** Cash benefits may not be reduced/suspended/withdrawn because the beneficiary resides in another member state.

## B. Applicable Legislation Rules (Art. 11–13) — the SMWE Determination Engine

### B.1 General Rule — Lex Loci Laboris (Art. 11(3))

| Worker type | Applicable legislation |
|---|---|
| Employed or self-employed in one state | That state |
| Civil servant | State of the employing administration |
| Receiving unemployment benefit under Art. 65 | Residence state |
| Non-active (not working) | Residence state |

**For SMWE: Swiss lex loci laboris means AHV/ALV applies to all persons working in Switzerland, regardless of residence.**

### B.2 Posting / Entsendung (Art. 12) — SMWE Determination: A1 Posted Worker

**Conditions for home-state maintenance (all four must be met):**

| Condition | Test |
|---|---|
| Employer normally carries out substantial activities in home state | Not a letter-box; real business activity (Reg. 987 Art. 14(2)) |
| Work performed on employer's behalf | Not sub-posted to a third employer |
| Duration ≤ 24 months | Anticipated; extension possible under Art. 16 |
| Not replacing another posted person | No back-to-back postings for the same role |

**Output:** If all conditions met → A1 certificate issued by home-state authority → home-state legislation maintained → no Swiss AHV/ALV obligation.

**Duration extension beyond 24 months:** Art. 16 allows competent authority agreement (Verständigungsvereinbarung / accord amiable). CH practice: extensions up to 5–6 years possible on application to cantonal SVA/SAK + BSV/OFAS.

### B.3 Multi-State Activity (Art. 13) — SMWE Determination: Cross-Border Workers

**Art. 13(1) — Employee working in 2+ states:**

| Situation | Applicable legislation |
|---|---|
| Substantial activity (≥25%) in state of residence | Residence state |
| No substantial activity in residence state; one employer | Employer's seat state |
| No substantial activity in residence state; multiple employers in different states | Residence state |

**"Substantial part" test (Reg. 987 Art. 14(8)):**
- Criterion: working time AND/OR remuneration
- **<25% = indicator that substantial part is NOT performed in that state**
- **≥25% = multi-state determination required; applicable legislation must be formally established**

**SMWE operational thresholds:**

| Home-country work time | SMWE Action |
|---|---|
| < 25% | Swiss legislation maintained; document and monitor |
| ≥ 25% | Formal multi-state analysis required; apply for A1 under Art. 13 |
| > 49.9% | Legislation shifts to home country; Swiss AHV/ALV stops |

**For cross-border workers:** Even a single day of formal home-office creates a theoretical multi-state situation. The 25% threshold is the practical trigger for formal procedure.

### B.4 Exceptions / Art. 16 Agreements

- Two or more competent authorities may agree to derogate from Art. 11–15 in the interest of certain persons.
- Used to: extend postings beyond 24 months; cover specific assignment structures not fitting Art. 12.
- Application: employer + employee jointly apply to home-state competent authority, which contacts host-state authority.

## C. Implementing Regulation — Key Audit Tests (Reg. 987 Art. 14)

### "Posted" qualification (Art. 14(1)):
- Recruitment WITH A VIEW TO posting also qualifies, PROVIDED the employee is already subject to home-state legislation BEFORE the employment begins. CH rule of thumb: ≥ 1 month prior AHV affiliation.

### "Normally carries out substantial activities" (Art. 14(2)):
- Employer must perform substantial activities OTHER than pure internal management in the home state.
- All criteria characterising the business, suited to its real nature, are assessed.
- Guards against letter-box posting (a company existing solely to create posted-worker status).

### Multi-state "normally" (Art. 14(5)–(6)):
- Applies to simultaneous OR continuously alternating activities in different states.
- Marginal activities excluded from the multi-state determination.
- Permanent vs. ad-hoc/temporary character determines posting vs. multi-state status.

## D. A1 Application — SMWE Procedural Guide

### D.1 Application routes (Switzerland outbound)

| Treaty partner | Form | Application authority | Expected timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| EU/EFTA member state | EE0015 (A1) | Cantonal SVA / AK (Ausgleichskasse) | 2–4 weeks (vary by canton) |
| UK (post-Brexit) | Certificate of Coverage | Cantonal SVA/SAK | 2–4 weeks |
| Bilateral agreement country | Country-specific form | Cantonal SVA/SAK or ZAS centrally | 4–8 weeks |

### D.2 ALPS (Automated Legal Processing System)
- Electronic A1 application system operated by SVA/SAK
- Bulk applications supported for multi-country travellers
- Employer can apply on behalf of employee with employee consent

### D.3 Country-specific A1 requirements

| Country | A1 on-site requirement | Penalty for absence |
|---|---|---|
| Germany | Must be available on-site on demand | Fines up to EUR 30,000 per case |
| France | Must be carried or accessible digitally | Fines; work stoppage orders |
| Italy | Must be presented on request | Administrative fines |
| Austria | Must be available; random checks common | Fines; potential prosecution |
| Belgium | Electronic A1 via LIMOSA system required before work starts | Fines |

## E. Key Definitions for SMWE Determinations

| Term | Definition | SMWE relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Posting / Entsendung | Temporary work in another state while remaining with home employer | A1 / GM-006 |
| Multi-state activity | Habitually working in 2+ states | GM-006 / GM-003 |
| Substantial activity | ≥25% of working time / remuneration in a state | GM-003 threshold |
| Home state | State of the employer's registered seat | A1 issuing authority |
| Host state | State where work is temporarily performed | A1 destination |
| Cross-border worker | Lives in one state, works in another, returns daily or weekly | IMM-005 / GM-002 |
| Posted worker | Sent from home country to work temporarily in another | GM-001 / GM-006 |
